ESBA loan programs

The outer orbit: SBA loans, explained.

SBA loans take the longest to arrive and are the hardest to beat on terms. We'll tell you early whether you're a strong candidate, and which program fits.

The all-rounder

SBA 7(a)

The SBA's main program and its most flexible. It can cover almost any business purpose.

  • Working capital, equipment and inventory
  • Buying a business, or buying out a partner
  • Refinancing existing business debt
  • Terms up to 10 years, or 25 for real estate
Property and heavy equipment

SBA 504

Long-term, fixed-rate financing for the big fixed assets a business grows into.

  • Buying or building commercial property
  • Heavy machinery and long-life equipment
  • Down payments often around 10%
  • Terms of 10, 20 or 25 years
Quicker decisions

SBA Express

A streamlined 7(a) for smaller amounts, with a faster answer from the lender.

  • Loans up to $500,000
  • Set up as a term loan or a revolving line
  • Less paperwork than a standard 7(a)
  • Suits working capital and smaller projects

What SBA lenders look for

  • Strong credit
  • Two or more years in business
  • Full financials: tax returns and financial statements

SBA loans are made by SBA-approved lenders and partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration. Approval is up to the lender and the SBA. Orbit Advance is not affiliated with or endorsed by the SBA.

How do I know which program fits?

Start with your deadline. If you need money within days, look at a merchant cash advance or a line of credit. If you have a specific project and a few days to spare, a term loan usually costs less. If you can wait a month or more and your business is established, an SBA loan is usually the lowest-cost option. The funding planner narrows it down, or email us and we'll tell you.

Can I qualify for more than one program?

Often, yes. Many businesses qualify for two or three programs at once. We set them side by side so you can compare the real cost and the payments before you choose.

What paperwork should I have ready?

For a merchant cash advance or a line of credit, usually three to six months of business bank statements and a photo ID. Term loans and SBA loans typically also call for business tax returns and financial statements. An SBA lender may ask for a debt schedule and a short business plan as well.

How quickly can the money arrive?

A merchant cash advance can fund in as little as 24 to 48 hours. Lines of credit are often approved in one to three days, and term loans usually fund within a few business days. SBA loans are the slowest, at roughly 30 to 90 days, in exchange for the longest terms and some of the lowest rates.

Is a merchant cash advance a loan?

No. It is a purchase of a portion of your future receivables, priced with a factor rate rather than an interest rate. You see the total amount to be repaid before you sign.

Is Orbit Advance part of the SBA?

No. SBA loans are made by SBA-approved lenders and partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration. Orbit Advance is not affiliated with or endorsed by the SBA. We help you understand the programs and pursue the right one.

Does it cost anything to ask?

No. Getting in touch and reviewing your options is free, and you're under no obligation to accept an offer.

Is there an online application?

Not on this website. Start with an email to admin@orbitadvance.com and we'll take it from there.
